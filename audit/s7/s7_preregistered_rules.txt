S7 PRE-REGISTERED INTERPRETATION RULES
Written 2026-08-19 BEFORE any S7 performance result was computed or observed.

These rules MUST NOT change after results are seen.

Q1 LINEAR PROBE (J1C)
  AUC >= 0.70          strong linear diagnostic accessibility survives parameter-free aggregation
  0.60 <= AUC < 0.70   partial linear accessibility
  AUC <  0.60          weak linear accessibility
  A low Q1 AUC MUST NOT be described as irreversible information destruction. The probe
  measures LINEAR ACCESSIBILITY ONLY, through a fixed parameter-free operator, with linear
  classifiers. It says nothing about what a trained non-linear model could recover.

RANDOM ENCODER (J3)
  If the paired mean (O_B_h - P_B_h) >= 0.05 AUC, with the bootstrap 95% CI supporting a
  consistent effect (CI excluding 0), classify O-specific architecture differences as
  POTENTIALLY LOAD-BEARING. Otherwise classify as not demonstrated at this sample size.
  Comparisons are paired by IDENTICAL encoder seed. Independently sampled seed sets must
  never be compared.
  Weak random-encoder AUC means weak linear accessibility through RANDOM weights. It does
  NOT prove training cannot improve the representation.

h VERSUS z
  If a stable |h - z| >= 0.03 AUC difference holds across encoder seeds (paired, CI
  excluding 0), carry BOTH h and z forward into later evaluation as an explicit fork
  rather than choosing one.

REFERENCE POINTS (frozen, external to S7)
  FC-only nested-CV AUC 0.7565 ; site-held-out 0.7432 (S5.5)
  ALFF-only best 0.6497 ; ALFF added nothing beyond FC (S5.5)

SCOPE
  NO A-GCL training, view-learner training, memory-bank training or contrastive
  optimization is performed in S7. All encoders are RANDOM and UNTRAINED, run under
  eval(), with requires_grad disabled except in the explicit gradient-flow check.
