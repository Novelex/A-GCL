These rules must not change after results are seen.

DATA
  cohort: frozen 954 (455 ASD / 499 NC), M1_B graph cache (sha 312266b2...), 90 nodes,
  8100 signed PCC edges, diagonal retained. data_dense_v3.pt and norm_matrix NOT used.

PRETRAINING (self-supervised, mirrors production protocol)
  the encoder+view learner train on ALL 954 graphs WITHOUT labels (transductive SSL,
  exactly as the production script iterates its dataloader over the full dataset).
  Labels are never visible to training. This is recorded, not hidden.

LOCKED HYPERPARAMETERS (identical across P/O/C unless the contract itself differs)
  seed              20260818 (single pilot seed; multi-seed comes later if pilot is sane)
  epochs            200 (paper Sec 2.4) — NO early stopping, NO best-epoch selection
  optimizer         Adam, lr 5e-4 for BOTH model and view learner (all three contracts)
  batch_size        32 ; emb_dim 32 ; z dim 32 ; mlp_edge_model_dim 64
  reg_lambda 2.0 ; cr_lambda 0.4 ; max_length 256
  per-contract differences (temps, signs, mask, bank, arch flags) are the CONTRACTS
  THEMSELVES, frozen in S8_START_REPORT.md — they are the experiment, not free knobs.
  threads pinned to 1 -> bitwise-reproducible CPU training (proven in smoke).

EVALUATION
  representation: BOTH h [954,32] and z [954,32] extracted at the FINAL epoch only.
  classifier: linear SVM inside Pipeline(StandardScaler, LinearSVC), C grid
  {1e-4..1e2} chosen by inner StratifiedKFold(5) on training folds only.
  splits: the EXACT frozen S3C outer 5-fold splits (sha 28fed44d...). Nested CV only.
  metrics: pooled OOF ROC-AUC (primary), balanced accuracy, accuracy, sensitivity,
  specificity, bootstrap 95% CI; loss curves recorded every epoch.
  NO best-epoch selection, NO representation cherry-picking: h and z BOTH reported for
  ALL configs regardless of which looks better. NO threshold tuning (decision_function>0).
  Reference points (frozen): FC-only linear SVM 0.7565 / LOSO 0.7432; M1-only 0.6497;
  random-encoder h/z ~0.50; Q1 0.6397.

COMPUTE
  CPU (deterministic, 1 thread) — GPUs are saturated (47/48 busy) and the model is tiny;
  smoke measured ~0.1 s/step -> 6000 steps ≈ well under a 12 h wall. One SLURM job per
  contract, run in parallel. GPU is NOT used for the pilot; this is a measured decision.

WHAT THE PILOT CAN AND CANNOT SHOW
  It compares three faithful contracts on identical data/seed/epochs. One seed cannot
  establish superiority claims; it establishes training SANITY (losses move, no collapse,
  no NaN) and first-order effect sizes to size the follow-up. No winner is declared from
  the pilot alone.
