These rules must not change after results are seen.

BANNED PHRASES (not provable by these probes, must not appear in the S7.5 report):
  "hard A-GCL ceiling"
  "information permanently destroyed"
  "A-GCL cannot recover this"
  "A-GCL is only a regional-strength model"

WHAT THE ENCODER-STAGE PROBES MEASURE
  Every encoder-stage probe uses a RANDOM, UNTRAINED encoder under eval().
  A low AUC therefore means: WEAK LINEAR ACCESSIBILITY at that representation,
  through a linear classifier, at random initialization.
  It does NOT mean information was destroyed, and it places NO bound on what a
  trained encoder could represent.

WHAT THE PERMUTATION-INVARIANT FEATURES MEASURE
  Hand-designed permutation-invariant summaries (sorted strengths, FC eigenvalues,
  sorted Q1 channels) are BASELINES. They estimate how much diagnosis is linearly
  accessible THROUGH THOSE SPECIFIC SUMMARIES. They do NOT bound the class of all
  permutation-invariant neural networks, and they are not architectural ceilings.

PRE-POOLING vs POOLING
  If flattened final nodes beat h, the ONLY permitted statement is:
  "fixed-ROI regional information is more linearly accessible before
   permutation-invariant sum pooling at random initialization."
  No claim about training may be attached to it.

ROI-ALIGNMENT CONTROL
  Independent per-subject ROI permutation is a DIAGNOSTIC of dependence on consistent
  anatomical correspondence. It is NOT a biological model and not a claim about A-GCL.

SOURCE-TRUTH STATEMENTS THAT MUST BE MAINTAINED
  - A-GCL uses trainable edge-level augmentation.
  - ViewLearner consumes source and destination node embeddings and emits one edge logit,
    so the trained model CAN learn edge-specific structure, not only strength summaries.
  - global_add_pool makes the graph representation permutation-invariant.
  - Any paper ablation percentage not confirmed from the primary text/table is reported as
    UNKNOWN / NOT VERIFIED.
